About the game

What Flip Match is

Flip two cards. Same picture? It's a match. Different? Remember where they were and try again. Flip Match takes the memory game everyone already knows and gives it the care of a modern mobile game: glossy 3D picture cards, smooth flips with haptic feedback, light and dark mode, and boards from a 4-card warm-up for small children to an 88-card challenge.

  • Many ways to play: solo against your best time, against the computer at five levels, pass-and-play for up to 4 players, or online rooms joined with a 6-digit code, no account needed.
  • 60-level campaign where every stage has its own twist.
  • Find the Card: a ring of cards flips face-up for a moment, then a target appears and you tap its match before the timer runs out.
  • Daily Memory Score: a few boards each day give a score for the day, tiers from Bronze to Diamond, and a chart that shows memory improving week by week.
  • Made to feel good: works offline, pause any time, 100+ achievements and a record for every board size.
  • Free with no ads, so families can play without interruptions.
