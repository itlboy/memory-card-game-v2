What's new

Updates marked “Update” reach the app automatically — no store update needed.

A brand-new look

UpdateOctober 18, 2026
  • A whole new look: chunky 3D buttons and real-looking cards that are fun to tap.
  • Light and dark mode — it follows your phone, or pick one in Settings.
  • A furry friend joins the end of every game: a panda smiles when you win, a lion bawls when you lose, and two mice bump heads on a draw.
  • Points are now shiny coins; stars only rate campaign levels.

Sharper 3D card art & 12 new themes

UpdateOctober 15, 2026
  • Card pictures are now 3D emoji art from Google's Noto set, the same on every phone, tablet and computer.
  • Pictures stay crisp at every board size, and big boards load lighter images so play stays smooth.
  • 12 new card themes: flags of Asia, Europe, the Americas and Africa, bugs, pets, shapes and colors, and more.

A fresh campaign

UpdateOctober 15, 2026
  • The campaign now has 60 levels: every board size comes back five times, each with a tighter clock.
  • Stars now count memory slips — flipping a wrong card when you had already seen its match. Exploring is free.
  • A perfect game with no slips earns a shining rainbow fourth star.
  • Star goals are shown before, during and after every level.
  • Cleaner in-game bar on small iPhones, and a friendlier update notice with a 3-2-1 countdown.
  • New languages: Italian, Turkish and Traditional Chinese — now 15 in total.
