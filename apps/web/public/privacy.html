Flip Match is a memory card game. You can play it without an account, and we collect as little as we can. We do not show ads and we never sell your data. We use Google Analytics to understand, in aggregate, how the game is used — see “Third-party services” below.

Playing offline

Solo games, the campaign, games against the computer and games on one shared device are stored only on your device: scores, records, achievements, settings and unfinished games. Nothing is sent to us. Deleting the app (or clearing the website's data) removes them.

Playing online

When you create or join an online room, our server receives and stores:

  • the nickname and avatar you choose, and the room's game state;
  • a random identifier created by the app (not linked to your identity), used to count players and keep your seat if your connection drops;
  • a match history entry (scores, board size, time) when a game ends.

Players can only talk with preset emoji and preset phrases — there is no free-text chat. Rooms close when everyone leaves, and closed rooms are permanently deleted after 30 days. Match history is kept without names for statistics. Room codes and nicknames are visible to the other players in the room, and public rooms are listed in the lobby.

Optional account

Signing in with Apple or Google is optional. It is only used to sync your progress across devices and show you on the leaderboard. If you sign in, we store:

DataWhy
Account identifier from Apple or GoogleTo recognise you when you sign in again
Email address, if shared (Apple may give a private relay address)Account recovery — never used for marketing
Name and profile pictureShown on your profile and the leaderboard
Scores, records, achievements, total pointsSync and leaderboard

You can delete your account at any time inside the app (Account → Delete account). This permanently erases your account, records and achievements from our server; for Apple accounts we also revoke the app's access to your Apple ID. Progress saved on your device is not affected.

Third-party services

  • Apple and Google handle sign-in under their own privacy policies (Apple, Google).
  • Our servers run on our own infrastructure and on Cloudflare, which processes network traffic (including IP addresses) to deliver and protect the service.
  • Google Analytics measures how the game and these pages are used, in aggregate: pages and screens viewed, games started and finished, device type, app version and approximate region. It never receives your name, email or account. Advertising features are turned off, and for visitors in Europe it runs without cookies. Google processes this data under its privacy policy; you can opt out with Google’s opt-out add-on.

Children

Flip Match is made for all ages, including children. Every mode can be played without an account, and online play asks only for a nickname — no name, email or other personal information. Signing in is optional and handled by Apple or Google. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security and storage

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS and WSS). Our server is located in Vietnam; Cloudflare may process traffic worldwide.

Your rights and contact

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data at any time by writing to support@cozypi.com. If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
