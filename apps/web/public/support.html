Frequently asked questions

Is Flip Match free?

Yes. Every mode is free to play, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

How do I play with friends online?

Tap “Play Online”, create a room, then share the 6-digit code or the invite link. Friends enter the code or open the link to join — up to 4 players. Public rooms also appear in the lobby list.

I lost connection during an online game.

Just reopen the app — your seat is kept for 5 minutes and you rejoin the same game automatically. While your connection is down, your turn timer pauses for a while so you don't lose your turn.

How do campaign stars work?

Stars count memory slips: flipping the wrong card when you had already seen its match. Exploring cards you have never seen is free. Fewer slips mean more stars, and a game with no slips at all earns a rainbow fourth star. Time does not change your stars — but if the clock runs out, the level is lost.

How do I change the language?

Open the menu (☰) in the top-right corner and pick one of the 15 languages. On iPhone and Android the app starts in your phone's language.

Will I lose my progress if I reinstall?

Without an account, progress lives only on your device and is removed with the app. Sign in with Apple or Google to keep it in sync across devices.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and its data from our server. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad, Android phones and tablets, and any modern web browser on a computer or phone.
