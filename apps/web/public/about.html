Ways to play

Something for every mood

Quick five-minute rounds, a long climb, or a noisy game night — Flip Match fits them all.

🗺️

60-level campaign

Twelve board sizes from 4 to 88 cards, each coming back five times with a tighter clock. Collect up to three stars — and a rainbow fourth star for a perfect memory.

🤖

Five computer opponents

From Wobbly Chick to Divine Dragon, each opponent remembers a little more than the last. Beat them all.

🌐

Online with friends

Create a room, share the 6-digit code and play with up to 4 people. Chat with emoji and friendly preset phrases — no open chat, so it stays kind.

👨‍👩‍👧

One phone, four players

Pass the phone around the table. Turns rotate fairly and the scoreboard keeps everyone honest.

🎴

48 themes, your rules

Animals, food, flags, sports and more — mix them freely. Tune time, lives, preview, shuffles and special cards.

🏅

Achievements to chase

Over 100 achievements reward streaks, speed, perfect games and big boards. Sign in to keep them on every device.
