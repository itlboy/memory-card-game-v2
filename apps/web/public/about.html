A memory card game for one to four players

Flip two cards at a time and find the matching pairs. Flip Match has a 60-level campaign, five computer opponents and online rooms for up to 4 players.

Ways to play

Modes and features

Play alone, against the computer, with others on one device, or in an online room.

🗺️

60-level campaign

60 levels on twelve board sizes, from 4 to 88 cards. Each level has its own rule: preview, shuffle, lives, special cards or a tighter clock. A level gives up to three stars, plus a rainbow fourth star for a game with no memory slips.

🤖

Five computer opponents

Five levels, from Wobbly Chick to Divine Dragon. Each one remembers more cards than the one before.

🌐

Online with friends

Create a room, share the 6-digit code and play with up to 4 people, each for themselves or in teams. Chat is limited to emoji and 12 preset phrases; there is no free-text chat.

👨‍👩‍👧

One phone, four players

Two to four players take turns on the same device. Any seat can be given to a bot.

🎴

Card themes and rules

12 themes, including Animals, Food and World Flags; a board can mix several. Time, Lives, Preview, Shuffle and Special Cards each have four settings.

🏅

Achievements

More than 90 achievements for combos, fast games, perfect games, big boards and more. Sign in to sync them across devices.
